## Wooden Ships & Iron Men

A browser version of the board game [Wooden Ships & Iron Men](
https://boardgamegeek.com/boardgame/237/wooden-ships-and-iron-men) (WS&IM).
Please read more on [github](
https://github.com/uablrek/hex-games/blob/main/ws-im/README.md).

# Scenarios

* **[Mars v.s. Hercule](ws-im.html?sc=mar-her&ai=solo&player=fr)** -
    Original sceanrio 9. A duel between 2 SOL2 ships. The scenario is
    played against the AI, and is not even. I have never won as the
    French player (Hercule), which is the default, but you can play as
    the [British](ws-im.html?sc=mar-her&ai=solo&player=br).

* **[A Nordic encounter](ws-im.html?sc=nordic&ai=solo&player=se)** - A
    fictional scenario where 2 British ships meets a Danish and a
    Swedish. The game is fairly even and is played against the AI. By
    default you (human) plays the Nordic side, but you can play as the
	[British](ws-im.html?sc=nordic&ai=solo&player=br).

* **[Trafalgar](ws-im.html?sc=trafalgar)** - Original scenario 15.
    Since ships arriving at later turns is not implemented yet,
    this is more of a solitarie (non-AI) example.

* **[Trafalgar extended](ws-im.html?sc=trafalgar2)** - An extended
    version of the above. The map is expanded so all ships can be
    deployed from start. Also French ships Achille and Agronaute are
    moved into the French/Spanish line.

* **[Battle of the Nile](ws-im.html?sc=nile)** - Original scenario 11.
    This scenario is a solitarie (non-AI) example with land. As
    with Trafalgar, ships arriving at later turns are not implemented.

* **[User Defined](ws-im.html?sc=user)** - If the game is loaded
    locally (from a `file://` URL) you may create your own scenario,
    otherwise this is the "test-scenario"
